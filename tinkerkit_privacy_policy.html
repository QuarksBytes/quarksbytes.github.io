Introduction

Sy System ("we," "our," or "us") operates Tinkerkit, an Arduino simulator mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use our App.

By downloading, installing, or using Tinkerkit, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will request it as necessary.

About Tinkerkit

Tinkerkit is an Arduino simulator designed to help users explore electronics, experiment with circuit designs, and learn about microcontrollers and electronic components in a simulated environment.

Depending on the features available in the App, users may be able to create virtual circuits, connect components, write or edit code, and simulate electronic projects without requiring physical Arduino hardware.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

Tinkerkit does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or postal address to use its core simulation features.

We do not intentionally collect personal information directly through the App unless a specific feature or third-party service requires it and the collection is disclosed as appropriate.

Circuit Designs, Code, and Project Data

Information such as circuit configurations, selected electronic components, source code, project settings, and simulation results may be processed or stored locally on your device to provide the simulator's functionality.

Unless the App provides an online synchronization, sharing, backup, or cloud-storage feature, projects are intended to remain on your device. If online features are introduced or enabled, relevant information may be processed by the services supporting those features.

Users should avoid including passwords, access tokens, personal information, or other confidential information in project code or circuit descriptions.

Google AdMob and other services integrated into the App may automatically collect or process certain technical information, depending on their configuration and your privacy choices. This information may include:

  • Device type and model

Where required by applicable law, the App will request consent before enabling relevant advertising or data processing activities. You may be able to manage advertising preferences through the App, your device settings, or available consent tools.

Core simulation activities and project processing may take place locally on your device, depending on the features and implementation of the App.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored projects and settings. We recommend exporting or backing up important projects before uninstalling the App or resetting your device.
