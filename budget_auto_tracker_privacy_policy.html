Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Introduction

Quarks Bytes ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Budget Auto Tracker mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used, and the choices you have when you use the App.

By downloading, installing, or using Budget Auto Tracker, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

About Budget Auto Tracker

Budget Auto Tracker is an Android application that helps you track your spending. With your permission, the App reads bank transaction alert messages (SMS) stored on your device and turns them into a list of transactions. You can also add transactions and money you have lent to others by hand. All of this is done locally on your device. The App does not require account registration, and Quarks Bytes does not operate any servers that receive your financial information.

Summary

  • Your SMS messages and transaction data are processed and stored only on your device.
  • We do not receive, store, sell, or share your messages, transactions, or any other personal information.
  • The App has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking services.
  • The only information the App requests from the internet is currency exchange rates.

Information the App Processes

SMS Messages

If you grant the SMS permission, the App reads messages in your device's SMS inbox in order to find bank and card transaction alerts (for example, messages stating that an amount was debited, spent, or credited). Messages that are not recognised as transaction alerts, including messages that contain one-time passwords (OTPs), are not saved by the App. Messages are read on your device only and are never transmitted to Quarks Bytes or to any third party.

Transaction Data Created From Messages

For each transaction alert it recognises, the App stores the following on your device:

  • Amount and currency
  • Payee or sender name as it appears in the message
  • Bank name and the last few digits of the account or card number shown in the message
  • Whether the message relates to a credit card, debit card, or bank account
  • Date and time of the transaction
  • The text of the transaction alert message itself, kept so the App can improve how it reads that transaction

Information You Enter

Transactions you add or edit manually, notes, and records of money you have lent to other people (including the names you type, amounts, dates, and repayments) are stored on your device. Names and details of other people that you enter are stored only on your device and are not shared with us.

App Settings

Preferences such as your chosen theme, base currency, selected filters, and whether the spending graph is shown are stored on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

Budget Auto Tracker does not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. The App does not include advertising services, analytics services, or crash-reporting services.

Permissions Used

Permission Why the App uses it
Read SMS (READ_SMS) To find bank and card transaction alerts in your inbox and turn them into transactions. This is the main purpose of the App. The permission is requested by the App itself and you can withdraw it at any time in your device's settings. If you decline or withdraw it, the App remains usable for adding transactions and lent money by hand.
Internet (INTERNET) To download current currency exchange rates so that amounts in foreign currencies can be converted into your base currency.

Use of SMS Data

SMS data is used only to provide the App's core feature: listing and summarising your bank transactions on your device. We do not use SMS data for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not transfer it to Quarks Bytes or to any third party. The App does not send your messages or anything derived from them off your device.

Internet Use and Exchange Rates

To convert foreign currency amounts, the App periodically requests exchange rates from a third-party exchange-rate service (open.er-api.com, operated by ExchangeRate-API). The request contains no information about your messages, transactions, or identity. As with any internet request, the service can see technical information such as your IP address, which it handles under its own privacy practices. If you are offline, the App uses the most recently saved rates.

How We Use Information

Because your data stays on your device, Quarks Bytes does not use it. On your device, the App uses the information described above only to show you your transactions, totals, graphs, card filters, and money lent, and to convert currencies.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which is protected by the Android operating system's app sandbox and by your device's screen lock and encryption settings. The App does not add its own separate encryption, so keeping your device locked and up to date helps protect this information.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for the security of your own device.

Android Backup

Depending on your device settings, Android's built-in backup feature may include the App's data in your Google account backup. Any such backup is handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy and is not accessible to Quarks Bytes. You can control this in your device's backup settings.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it, because we do not receive it. We do not have access to your messages, transactions, or lent-money records and are therefore unable to disclose them to anyone.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • Delete an individual transaction or lent-money record inside the App (swipe it or open it and choose delete)
  • Delete all App data by clearing the App's storage in your device's Settings
  • Remove everything by uninstalling the App
  • Withdraw the SMS permission at any time in your device's Settings

Deleting a transaction inside the App does not delete the original SMS message from your inbox. Quarks Bytes holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's Privacy

Budget Auto Tracker is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

International Users

The App works the same way wherever you use it. Your messages and transactions remain on your device. The only information that may cross borders is the exchange-rate request described above.
