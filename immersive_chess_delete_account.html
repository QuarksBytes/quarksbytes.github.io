Delete your account and data

Immersive Chess for Android · by Sy System · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Immersive Chess has no accounts of its own. The only sign-in in the app is the optional “Sign in with Lichess” for online play. We do not run a server that stores your account, games or profile, so there is nothing about you on our side to delete.

1. Remove your Lichess sign-in from the app

  1. Open Immersive Chess.
  2. Tap Lichess Online on the home screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Sign out.

Signing out revokes the app’s access token on Lichess and deletes it from your device’s secure storage. You can also revoke the app’s access at any time from lichess.org/account/security.

2. Delete your Lichess account

Your Lichess account belongs to Lichess.org, not to us. To close it, sign in on the Lichess website and go to lichess.org/account/close. Lichess then handles your data under the Lichess privacy policy.

3. Delete data stored on your device

Saved games, settings, puzzle progress and unlocked armies are stored only on your device. Delete saved games inside the app, or uninstall the app (or use Settings → Apps → Immersive Chess → Storage → Clear data) to remove everything.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted immediately: the Lichess sign-in token and username on your device (on sign out), and all local app data (on uninstall or clearing storage).
  • Kept by us: nothing. We do not store your Lichess data or account on any server.
  • Kept by others: anonymous analytics (Google Firebase) and advertising data (Google AdMob) are kept by Google for a limited period under its own retention policies and are not linked to your Lichess account. Your “Remove Ads” purchase record is kept by Google Play.
